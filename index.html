      Neighborhood consistency

      Flags accounts whose value differs from what most comparable properties around them carry. Neighbors are parcels within ~60 ft (across a typical road); stacked condo units count as neighbors of each other. For utilities, vacant lots are compared with vacant lots and improved with improved.

      About this dashboard

      An internal tool for the Gunnison County Assessor's Office. It reads land attributes, land details, values and sales from RealWare through saved ListBuilder searches, refreshed weekly and on demand. Parcel shapes come from the county tax-parcel GIS layer. RealWare remains the system of record.

      Data

      Appraisal periods

      Gunnison County reappraises real property every odd year. Values reflect market conditions on June 30 of the prior year, using a minimum of 24 months of qualified sales (July 1 three years before through June 30 of the year before). Sale labels show the most recent qualified sale inside the selected period, which can be extended back in 6-month steps.
